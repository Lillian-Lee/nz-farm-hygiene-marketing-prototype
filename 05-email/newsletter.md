## How this email is measured

**Primary (engagement):** open rate (with A/B subject-line winner), click rate, click-to-open rate, top-clicked link.
**Secondary (demand signals):** product-page visits and add-to-cart attributed to the email (GA4 UTM: `utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch`), stockist replies.
**Deliverability guardrails:** bounce rate, spam complaints, unsubscribe rate.

**Explicitly not measured as "sales success" from a prototype** — this is a leading-indicator test of message and list health, not a revenue claim.

## A/B test plan
- Test the two subject lines on a small split; send the winner to the remainder.
- Hypothesis: the curiosity/education subject (A) beats the brand subject (B) on open rate for this advice-led audience.
