# 7-Day Content Calendar — HerdGuard prototype

> Portfolio prototype. A one-week launch-week sample across organic social + one email touch. Times are NZ. Channels: Facebook (FB), Instagram (IG), Email.

| Day | Channel | Format | Theme / hook | CTA | Primary measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | FB + IG | Feed image | **Brand intro** — "New here: farm hygiene that comes with the routine, not just the bottle." | Follow / Learn more | Reach, follows |
| Tue | IG | Reel (15s) | **Teat-spray routine** — every teat, every cow, every milking; aim for full coverage. | Save this / Shop teat spray | Saves, video views |
| Wed | FB | Link post | **Blog teaser** — "How much emollient does teat spray actually need?" (15–20%) | Read the routine | Link CTR, LP views |
| Thu | Email | Newsletter | **Welcome / launch** email (see `05-email`) — who we are, the 2 products, free delivery over $150. | Shop the range | Open rate, click rate |
| Fri | FB + IG | Carousel | **Biosecurity basics** — footbaths at every entry point; clean before you disinfect. | Shop disinfectant | Swipes, link CTR |
| Sat | IG | Story (poll) | **Engagement** — "What's your teat-spray setup: RTU or mix-your-own?" poll. | Reply / DM | Poll responses, replies |
| Sun | FB + IG | Feed image | **Social proof / stockist** — "Rural retailer? Become a HerdGuard stockist." | Become a stockist | Enquiries |

## Weekly cadence logic

- **Educate before selling.** Mon–Wed build credibility (routine, emollient, brand) so the Thu email and Fri product push land with an audience that already trusts the advice.
- **One email, well-timed.** Thursday mid-week tends to suit rural inboxes; the email consolidates the week's message.
- **Two-way engagement** (Sat poll) feeds audience insight and the algorithm without a hard sell.
- **Dealer ask** on Sunday keeps the B2B/stockist path visible without dominating.

## Notes for a real run

- Batch-produce creatives at the start of the week.
- Repurpose the Tue Reel as a Meta ad variant if it performs organically.
- Keep a swipe file of real farmer language from comments to refine copy.
- Nothing here is scheduled to auto-publish in the prototype — it's a plan, not a live queue.
