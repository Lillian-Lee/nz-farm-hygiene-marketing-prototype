🛡 HerdGuard — NZ Farm-Hygiene Ecommerce Marketing Prototype

An end-to-end digital-marketing portfolio project: a Shopify-style store, SEO, a Meta campaign, email and a GA4 measurement plan for a fictional New Zealand dairy & poultry hygiene brand.
Portfolio piece · July 2026
📁 PORTFOLIO PROTOTYPE. HerdGuard is a made-up brand. Not a real store; not affiliated with any real company. No ads were run, no spend, no real sales — all metrics shown are illustrative placeholders.

The brief I set myself

Show the full ecommerce loop a digital marketer runs — build the store, get it found, drive traffic, capture the audience, and prove I can measure it — using the NZ farm-hygiene audience's own language.

The audience & their words

NZ dairy (primary) and poultry (secondary) farmers. Copy uses their vocabulary sourced from DairyNZ & MPI: teat spray, emollient, cups off, coverage, SCC, footbath, biosecurity. Pain points: mastitis & high cell counts, poor teat condition, inconsistent spray coverage, keeping disease out.

Two products

  • Teat Spray Concentrate — emollient-rich, dairy.
  • Shed & Footbath Disinfectant — broad-spectrum, dairy + poultry biosecurity.

What I built

StoreHome, collection & 2 product pages (Shopify-style), desktop + mobile
SEOTitles & meta descriptions, keyword + internal-link map, checklist
Meta adsCampaign brief + 2 A/B ad concepts (mock-ups) — drafts only, no spend
Content7-day calendar + 3 social posts
EmailSandbox launch newsletter (A/B subject lines, CTA, measures)
AnalyticsGA4 event spec + funnel + 1-page dashboard

Measurement thinking

Keep awareness (reach, sessions), engagement (view_item, add_to_cart, email CTR) and sales (checkout, purchase, stockist leads) as separate questions — never one blended number. UTMs on every paid/email link for attribution.
