We sell the routine, not just the bottle

Product only works if it's used right. Every HerdGuard page includes the practical routine — because coverage and consistency are where most gains (and losses) actually happen.

Teat health

Spray every teat, every cow, every milking

Apply immediately after cups come off, all through lactation, aiming for full coverage — the step most often "poorly done".

Mixing

15–20% emollient, mixed fresh

Keep emollient at 15–20% of the final mix (higher in high-challenge times) and remake every 1–3 days with drinking-quality water.

Biosecurity

Footbaths at every entry point

People, vehicles and equipment carry disease. Keep charged footbaths at shed doors and gates and refresh them regularly.
