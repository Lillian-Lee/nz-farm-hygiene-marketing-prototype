How to use — the routine

Remove organic matter and clean surfaces first — disinfectant does little on a dirty surface. Mix to the label rate with clean water, apply and allow the full contact time. For footbaths, place at shed doors and farm gates so people, vehicles and equipment pass through, and refresh the solution regularly (and whenever visibly dirty). Keep records as part of your biosecurity plan.
