# Two Meta Ad Variants — HerdGuard prototype

> Portfolio prototype. Draft ad concepts for a fictional brand. **Not published, no spend.** Visual mock-ups are in `ad-mockups/` (see screenshots). A/B intent: same audience and objective, different message angle.

## Shared setup
- **Objective:** Awareness / Traffic (test), later Sales.
- **Audience:** Segment A (dairy) primary; test Segment B (poultry) separately.
- **Placements:** FB + IG feed (1:1), Stories/Reels (9:16).
- **Landing page:** relevant product page.
- **Success:** cost per result, link CTR, landing-page views, add-to-cart/enquiry (GA4) — awareness first, not sales.

---

## Variant A — Problem / Solution (teat condition)

**Angle:** Speak to the spring/cold pain point — cracked, chapped teats and the mastitis risk that follows.

- **Primary text:**
  > Spring mud and cold snaps are hard on teat skin — and rough, cracked teats are exactly where mastitis starts. HerdGuard teat spray is emollient-rich (15–20%) to help keep skin soft and supple, milking after milking. Get the coverage right and you're protecting both your cows and your milk quality. 🐄
- **Headline:** `Protect teat condition this season`
- **Description:** `Emollient-rich teat spray · RTU or concentrate · Free rural delivery over $150`
- **CTA button:** Shop now
- **Creative:** Product + "soft, supple teats" visual; on-image text "15–20% emollient".

---

## Variant B — Routine / Education (coverage)

**Angle:** Lead with the free advice ("we sell the routine"), lower the guard, then introduce the product.

- **Primary text:**
  > The teat-spray step most sheds get wrong? Coverage. Spray every teat, every cow, immediately after cups come off — all through lactation. Do that consistently and good teat spraying is linked to around half the new mastitis infections. Our spray is built to make full coverage easy. Here's the routine 👇
- **Headline:** `The 4-point teat-spray routine`
- **Description:** `Made for full coverage · Mix fresh, 15–20% emollient · Shop HerdGuard`
- **CTA button:** Learn more
- **Creative:** 4-step routine graphic (matches the Reel), HerdGuard bottle at the end.

---

## What we'd test
- **Message:** pain-point (A) vs education (B) — which earns cheaper attention and better landing-page engagement.
- **CTA:** "Shop now" (A) vs "Learn more" (B).
- **Creative format:** static (A) vs step-graphic/video (B).

## What we would NOT claim
- No "HerdGuard reduces mastitis by X%" — the stat is a *practice* benefit (DairyNZ).
- No sales/ROI claims from a prototype.
- No paid results — nothing was run.
