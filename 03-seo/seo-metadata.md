# SEO Page Metadata — HerdGuard prototype

> Portfolio prototype for a fictional NZ farm-hygiene brand. Titles are kept under ~60 characters and meta descriptions under ~155 characters where possible, written in the audience's own vocabulary (teat spray, emollient, footbath, biosecurity).

## Home — `index.html`

- **Title:** `HerdGuard | Farm Hygiene for NZ Dairy & Poultry Farmers`
- **Meta description:** `Practical hygiene products for New Zealand dairy and poultry farms — teat spray, shed and footbath disinfectant. Trusted routines, fast rural delivery.`
- **H1:** Farm hygiene that keeps your herd healthy and your grades high.
- **Primary keyword:** farm hygiene NZ
- **Secondary:** dairy hygiene products, poultry biosecurity

## Collection — `collection.html`

- **Title:** `Shop Farm Hygiene | Dairy & Poultry | HerdGuard`
- **Meta description:** `Shop HerdGuard farm-hygiene products for NZ dairy and poultry: emollient teat spray and broad-spectrum shed and footbath disinfectant. Bulk & concentrate sizes.`
- **H1:** Farm Hygiene — Dairy & Poultry
- **Primary keyword:** farm hygiene products NZ
- **Secondary:** teat spray, farm disinfectant

## Product — Teat spray — `product-teat-spray.html`

- **Title:** `HerdGuard Teat Spray Concentrate | Emollient Teat Spray NZ`
- **Meta description:** `Emollient-rich post-milking teat spray for NZ dairy herds. Helps protect teat condition and support milk quality. RTU or concentrate, 20L & 200L.`
- **H1:** HerdGuard Teat Spray Concentrate
- **Primary keyword:** teat spray NZ
- **Secondary:** emollient teat spray, post milking teat spray, mastitis prevention teat spray

## Product — Disinfectant — `product-disinfectant.html`

- **Title:** `HerdGuard Shed & Footbath Disinfectant | Farm Disinfectant NZ`
- **Meta description:** `Broad-spectrum concentrate disinfectant for NZ dairy and poultry sheds and footbaths. Supports cleaning, disinfection and biosecurity. 20L & 200L.`
- **H1:** HerdGuard Shed & Footbath Disinfectant
- **Primary keyword:** farm disinfectant NZ
- **Secondary:** footbath disinfectant, poultry shed disinfectant, biosecurity disinfectant

## Structured data (recommended, not yet implemented in prototype)

Add JSON-LD `Product` schema on each product page (name, description, brand, offers/price, availability) and `Organization` schema on the home page. This is listed as a next step in the SEO checklist rather than built into the static prototype.
