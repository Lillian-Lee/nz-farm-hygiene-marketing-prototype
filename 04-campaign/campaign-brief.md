# Meta Campaign Brief — HerdGuard "Protect Your Teat Condition" (prototype)

> Portfolio prototype. This is a **draft brief for a fictional brand**. No campaign was published and no ad spend occurred. Any "draft in Ads Manager" step would require account access and a separate go/no-go decision before a single dollar is spent.

## 1. Objective

Build **awareness and consideration** of HerdGuard among NZ dairy (primary) and poultry (secondary) farmers, and capture early demand via product-page visits, add-to-cart and stockist enquiries. This is an awareness-led launch — the goal is not to claim immediate sales, but to seed the audience and learn which message and creative earn attention cheaply.

**Meta objective mapping:**
- Primary campaign: **Awareness / Reach** (or Traffic) to test messaging at low cost.
- Secondary campaign (once a message wins): **Sales / Conversions** optimised to add-to-cart and enquiry events (see GA4 plan).

## 2. Audience

**Segment A — Dairy (primary).** NZ-based, interests/behaviours around dairy farming, DairyNZ, Fieldays, agriculture, farm supplies; age 25–64; often rural locations (Waikato, Canterbury, Southland, Taranaki). Message angle: teat condition, milk quality, mastitis prevention routine.

**Segment B — Poultry / mixed & lifestyle (secondary).** Interests around poultry, biosecurity, lifestyle blocks, backyard/commercial hens; heightened by avian-influenza awareness. Message angle: shed cleaning, footbaths, keeping disease out.

Exclude existing purchasers/subscribers from awareness sets once retargeting is live.

## 3. Positioning & key message

> **"We sell the routine, not just the bottle."**
> Emollient-rich teat spray and broad-spectrum disinfectant, plus the practical routines that make them work — from people who understand NZ farming.

Proof points (used carefully): good teat-spray practice cuts new mastitis infections ~50% (DairyNZ, framed as a *practice* benefit); 15–20% emollient for teat condition; footbaths at every entry point for biosecurity. No product-specific efficacy claims without evidence.

## 4. Offer / CTA

- Awareness ads: "Learn more" → product/advice page.
- Consideration ads: "Shop now" → product page; free rural delivery over $150.
- Dealer angle (separate ad set): "Become a stockist" → trade form.

## 5. Creative

Two variants (see `meta-ad-variants.md` and the `ad-mockups/` screenshots): one **problem/solution** (teat condition) and one **routine/education** (spray every cow, every milking). Square (1:1) and vertical (4:5 / 9:16 for Reels/Stories) recommended.

## 6. Budget & flighting (illustrative)

- Test phase: small daily budget across 2 creatives × 2 segments for ~10–14 days.
- Keep, cut or iterate based on cost-per-result and landing-page engagement — **not** on sales alone during awareness.
- **No spend without a separate, explicit decision.** Figures here are placeholders for the portfolio.

## 7. How success is judged (tie to measurement plan)

- **Awareness:** reach, frequency, cost per 1,000 reached, 3-sec/thruplays.
- **Engagement:** link CTR, landing-page views, product engagement events (GA4).
- **Demand signals:** add-to-cart and stockist enquiries (GA4) — leading indicators, not a sales claim.
See `06-measurement/ga4-event-plan.md` for the full split of awareness vs engagement vs sales measures.
