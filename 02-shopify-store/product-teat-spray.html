Teat spraying after every milking is shown to reduce new mastitis infections by around 50% as a practice (DairyNZ). This is a benefit of good teat-spray routine, not a product-specific claim. Results depend on coverage, mixing and milking management. Always follow label directions.

How to use — the routine

Spray all teats of every cow immediately after the cups come off, right through lactation, aiming for complete coverage. If mixing from concentrate, keep emollient at 15–20% of the final mix (higher during high-challenge times), remake fresh every 1–3 days, and use drinking-quality water. Store out of direct sun and frost.
